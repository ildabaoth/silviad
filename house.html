Menandros Silviades

the father, drowned

Ran the house and kept the log before his son did. Traded as Menandara. Kept a chest that no one in the household was permitted to open, and opened it, in the end, in the water.

Eudokia

the mother, dead

Hellenic, of a second family that had come east a generation behind his. Died when the boy was four. The household stopped saying her name — not from grief, from arithmetic. The boy was named for her.

Edouinos

the son

Sixteen. Can get a price down, can take a knock, can read four alphabets and count better than anyone in his company. Has never won a fight worth describing and does not pretend otherwise.

Shiriko'omaro

caravan master

Thirty-nine, of the shore. He decides what the company carries, who carries it, and when it moves — which, on a beach in January, is the only office that means anything.

Shiina

second

Twenty-two, and called the Noble. He does the talking; the master does not. The name came with him and the log never says from where.

Tsunenao

physician

Forty-four, and the first of them who was not on that beach on the second day. He joined the company on the road south in the year 867, for reasons of his own, and has been treating all of them whether or not they consented ever since.
