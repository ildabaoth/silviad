Volume I of The Silviad

The Long Road West

866 – 883

Kept by Edouinos Silviades. In this volume: Edouinos Silviades, Shiriko'omaro, Tokifusa, and then Matako.

Begun at Chupka on 1 January 867. Opens with the father's leaves at Naniwa, because the boy who inherits them cannot begin anywhere else. Closes at Constantinople in the third month of 883: the road west is finished, the company is turned toward the Macedonian hills, and its captain holds no land.

The Storm

866 – 867 · in the hand of Edouinos Silviades

Late December, the northern sea. A trading house's last voyage, a wreck, and a confession made too late to argue with. Ends on 1 January 867, on a northern shore, with one survivor, a ledger, a chest, and nobody to hear the names but the fire.

The Long Detour

January 867 – May 871 · in the hand of Shiriko'omaro

Five years, and not one of them spent going west. The salvage winter and the naming of the company; the road south through Japan with a chest and a ledger; the first crossing to the island kingdom; a contract too good to refuse that drags them back north to a winter at Kushiro; and then the long way round — by sea to the mainland, down the coast, west across the steppe, into Tang, and out again the wrong way. It ends with two years in a school in the far south-west, where the books he carried in a chest become an argument he can win. May 871, on the road out of China: west at last, four and a half years late, solvent, and educated.
