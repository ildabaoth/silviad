Volume VI of The Silviad

The Sister’s Book

929 – 932.5

Kept and written by Philostrate.

Opened at Regensburg in the third month of 929, where Volume V closed: the ledger passes to Philostrate, sixteen, the King’s sister, the first of this house to hold the pen. She sees the realm as accounts, alliances and people, and she writes from the inside of the household. The record stops at Spytihněv in Moravia on the eleventh day of the fifth month of 932, on the road, in her hand. Two chapters: the fourth Chalcedonian rising beaten at Würzburg with the largest army either king led in the record, and the death of Queen Raina, the old world dying around the King.

Part I

Nitra

929 – 930
1

Nitra

929 \u2013 930 · in the hand of Philostrate

Philostrate, sixteen, the King's sister, opens the sixth volume. On the sixteenth day of the third month of 929 Antiphates realizes Dusica is his soulmate, the same word the record gave Edouinos and Raina in 907. Three days later, on the nineteenth, he declares war on Nitra against Dušan of Great Moravia — the fourth of the four wars of conquest. The war runs ten months and ten days and the record gives no battle for it. On the twenty-second day of the twelfth month Dusica bears Nikanor, her fourth child. On the twenty-ninth day of the first month of 930 Dušan is imprisoned — the second King of Great Moravia to be imprisoned in this book, after Dobroslav in 916 — and Nitra, Hont, Liptov and Gemer go to Kleanthes. On the first day of the second month five Moravian lords become vassals: Dobroslav, Spitihnev, Predslav, Zvonimír and Kleanthes. Dobroslav is the same King of Great Moravia Edouinos imprisoned in 916. He is alive as a vassal of the son. On the fourth day of the third month Radovan, of the company, flagellates himself to death. The realm reaches one hundred and five counties.

930.4 – 931.2

The Franconian Rising

930.4 – 931.2 · in the hand of Philostrate

Philostrate continues the sixth volume. On the twentieth day of the fourth month of 930 Ekbert declares the Franconian Chalcedonian Uprising — the fourth Chalcedonian rising against this house, after Heilwich in 908, Willichin in 921 and Dietrich in 923. Ten days later Dobroslav, King of Great Moravia and the first king imprisoned in this book, dies falling. On the eleventh day of the fifth month Euktemon dies on the march, his three counties passing to three heirs. On the fifteenth day of the sixth month the King destroys the whole enemy army at Würzburg — 8,313 against 4,029, with 132 lost — the largest army either king led in the record. Krapina and Fulda follow. At Siegen the King fights his own vassal Udo. On the sixteenth day of the eighth month the rising is beaten and seven of his own lords are imprisoned: Ladislav, Pribina, Spitihnev, Predslav, Zvonimír, Udo and Friedrica. The feast at Wien runs one hundred and thirty-two days. On the second day of the twelfth month Slavac dies, the last living walker of the 908 road to Rome besides the King. On the first day of the first month of 931 Lealdo and Lysistratos become champions and Württemberg and Zollern join the realm, bringing it to one hundred and seven counties.

Part III

Raina

931.3 – 932.5
3

Raina

931.3 – 932.5 · in the hand of Philostrate

Philostrate closes the book. On the third day of the fifth month of 931 Queen Raina dies of a heart attack, at fifty-eight — the old king's wife from 901, his soulmate from 907, the King's mother, the Queen who stayed at the gate in 908. The record calls the King's memory of it Died, one word. Five days later, on the eighth day, a son is born to Dusica and the King names him Antiphates. On the second day of the tenth month Ludwig the Black dies, once King of Bavaria, beaten and imprisoned by Edouinos at Lengenfeld in 907; Frankfurt leaves the realm and the count drops to one hundred and six. Friedrich the Forgetful dies on the twenty-sixth day of the first month of 932. Bratromila of Ráb, host of the 912 Recital, dies on the first day of the second month. King Uniedrog of Poland, host of the 915 Recital, dies on the nineteenth day of the third month. On the first day of the fourth month a second coronation begins, intent Bear Witness, unexplained. On the eleventh day of the fifth month the record stops at Spytihněv in Moravia, on the road, in Philostrate's hand. Here ends the book.
